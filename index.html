Welcome!

My name is Ariadna Albors Zumel, I am from Catalonia, and I am a PhD student in Sociology and Social Research at the University of Trento, Italy.

My research focuses on using advanced computational methods for crime research. Specifically, in my thesis, I have developed a deep learning model for crime forecasting at very fine spatiotemporal granularity, tested the fragility of the causal link between ambient population and crime, and measured bias in LLMs when used for threat detection.

Have a look at my publications or my CV, and feel free to get in touch.
