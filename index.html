Welcome!

My name is Ariadna Albors Zumel and I am a PhD student in Sociology and Social Research at the University of Trento, Italy. I am originally from Catalonia.

My research develops computational methods for the study of crime: forecasting spatiotemporal crime patterns with deep learning and human mobility data, testing the fragility of causal claims in urban environments, and measuring bias in large language models used for threat detection. I came to this through a somewhat winding path — astronomy, data science, and financial mathematics — and I still like working at the boundary between fields.

Have a look at my publications or my CV, and feel free to get in touch.
